What it does, in about 5 minutes
  1. Sets up the brain (OpenAI, Claude or a local model) and checks it works
  2. Finds Python, or installs it for you
  3. Installs Lazy-Monster in its own folder
  4. Downloads the voice models and learns your voice
  5. Starts it at sign-in and adds it to the Start menu
Prefer to read the script first?

Good habit. Open install.ps1 on GitHub, or download it and run it yourself. To remove everything later, run uninstall.ps1 from the install folder.

Questions people ask.

Is it listening all the time?

Only a small wake-word model listens, on your PC. Nothing is transcribed, saved or sent until you say "Hey Monster" or press Ctrl+Alt+Space.

What leaves my PC?

Your request as text, to plan bigger tasks with the model you choose. Speech recognition and the voice stay local. Research questions go to the web search you configure.

What does it cost?

The app is free. Bigger tasks use your own OpenAI or Claude key, usually a few cents at most; instant commands like "mute" cost nothing.

Can it run on a local model?

Yes. Point it at Ollama, llama.cpp, vLLM or LM Studio and nothing leaves your network. The installer finds them and checks the model can use tools. Web research still works through DuckDuckGo or Brave Search.

How do I change the brain or model?

Open the gear in the monster's window and pick OpenAI, Claude or a local model; the model list comes live from your key, so new models appear on their own. Or just say "Hey Monster, switch to Claude" or "use the bigger brain".

Which AI does it use?

Your choice of brain: OpenAI, Claude or a local model. Add Jev by TypeSafe and it makes the split-second calls around the brain: was that meant for me, which tool, did you say yes. Speech, voice and the wake word always run on your PC.

Can I use my own voice models?

Yes. Pick a built-in voice with "monster voices", or point it at your own Kokoro-format voice files, your own Whisper export, and retrain the wake word on your voice. The repo's docs/VOICES.md shows how.

Can it remind me?

Yes. "Remind me about the cafe meeting at 8 AM" and at 8 the monster wakes up on its own, tells you, and suggests what it could do next, like opening the project or drafting an email. It always asks first.

How do I update or remove it?

Run the install command again, or say "monster update" in a terminal. To remove it, run uninstall.ps1 from %LOCALAPPDATA%\LazyMonster\app. It asks before deleting your voice and settings.

Do I need an NPU?

No. With an Intel AI PC the wake word runs on the NPU and saves battery. Without one, it runs on the CPU.

Does it work on a Mac?

Next week, for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Watch the GitHub repo for releases and you'll hear the moment it lands.

Can it delete my files?

No. There is no delete, no terminal, no sending and no buying. It writes new files in its own folder and asks before anything risky.

What if someone else says "Hey Monster"?

With the voice lock on, it ignores voices that aren't yours. Typed requests and push-to-talk always work for you.
